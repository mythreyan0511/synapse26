A healthy society begins with healthy choices. As students and future healthcare professionals, we believe that our responsibility extends beyond the classroom and into the community.

Substance abuse can affect health, education, relationships, dreams and the future of an individual. Awareness, prevention and timely support can make a lasting difference.

Through SYNAPSE '26, we stand together to spread awareness, encourage healthy choices and inspire our generation to stay away from drugs and substance abuse.

THE INSTITUTION

GOVERNMENT
VELLORE MEDICAL COLLEGE

Established in 2005, Government Vellore Medical College is a government medical institution located at Adukkamparai, Vellore. The college is affiliated with The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University and functions alongside its teaching hospital on a unified campus.

CAMPUS 37.53 ACRES Unitary campus
ESTABLISHED 2005 Medical education since 2005
TEACHING HOSPITAL 1,500 BEDS Including teaching beds
SPECIALTY CARE 30+ CLINICS Multiple specialty services
OUTPATIENT CARE ~2,200 / DAY Approximate daily OPD
REGIONAL REACH REFERRAL CENTRE Serving neighbouring districts

THE SYNAPSE EXPERIENCE

WHERE
TALENT COMES ALIVE.

Synapse is the annual intercollegiate cultural and sports fest organized by Government Vellore Medical College.

It is a platform where creativity, talent, and competitive spirit come alive. Students from various institutions gather to showcase their best — from thrilling sportsmanship to dazzling performances.
